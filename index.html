Khan-Bong / 牵魂

《牵魂》是一款以中国神鬼文化为背景的回合制棋盘策略游戏。玩家无法直接观测敌人的位置，只能依靠 UI 提示、香火与环境反馈推断鬼魂所在，并规划下一步行动。

查看项目

Khan-Bong is a turn-based strategy game built around the insecurity and dread of navigating through darkness, where players infer invisible enemies through indirect feedback.

Gullen Theatre / 居伦剧院

根据迪伦马特《老妇还乡》改编的互动沉浸式戏剧，将原作中衰败的居伦小镇改写为一座在疫情冲击下挣扎求生的剧院。

An interactive immersive theatre adaptation of Dürrenmatt's The Visit, reframing Güllen as a theatre struggling to survive the pandemic.

Rigged Champion / 假赛拳王

将屏幕内的格斗与屏幕外对控制器的真实争抢结合在一起。玩家的目标并不是击败对手，而是在关键时刻设法输掉比赛。

查看项目

A two-player fighting game that blends on-screen competition with physical interaction around the controller, built around the unusual goal of trying to lose.

6 Mornings

通过六个看似相似、实际不断变化的清晨，让玩家从环境、对话和行为结果的细微差异中逐渐进入一位阿兹海默症老人的认知世界。

查看项目

A narrative experience using six subtly changing mornings to place the player inside the shifting perception of an elderly person living with Alzheimer's disease.

A live-action escape room in which players decipher an unfamiliar language and attempt to communicate with an alien researcher studying human civilization.
